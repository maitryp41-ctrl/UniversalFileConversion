      About

      Smart Universal File Converter converts files directly (images, documents, audio, video) and transforms content intelligently (PDF or image to MP3, MP3 or video to DOCX, ZIP to PDF).

      This is the prototype for Software Engineering Practical 9.

      Help

      1. Drag and drop a file, or choose one.
      2. Pick an output format under Direct or Advanced Conversion.
      3. Press Start Conversion and wait for the steps to finish.
      4. Download the converted file.

      Advanced conversions that use OCR or speech recognition depend on how clear the source is.
      Prototype: image-to-image and image-to-PDF run in your browser. Other conversions are simulated and download a demo text file.
